# Operator Response Guideline

## Verify before acting
Validate critical indications using a redundant measurement or field confirmation. Check whether the equipment is running, standby, or under maintenance. Review interlocks and the approved operating procedure before switching equipment.

## Communication
Notify the control-room lead for a verified critical recurring condition. Record the observed values and time. If decision-support evidence is incomplete, state the gap and continue through established operating procedures.

## AI decision support
The assistant can search assets, analyze alarms, retrieve maintenance history, find procedures, and draft an escalation. It cannot acknowledge, suppress, trip, start, stop, or isolate plant equipment.
