# Rotating Equipment Maintenance Playbook

## Repeated low pressure
For repeated low-discharge-pressure events, inspect suction restriction, recirculation leakage, impeller wear, instrument impulse-line blockage, and process demand changes. Compare the alarm chronology with completed work and operating-mode changes.

## Vibration relationship
High drive-end vibration after alignment work can indicate residual misalignment, soft foot, looseness, or bearing damage. Vibration and pressure symptoms may share a hydraulic cause such as cavitation, but correlation is not proof of causation.

## Ticket quality
A maintenance escalation should include asset identity, occurrence count, severity distribution, active condition, related work orders, cited procedure, recommended inspection, uncertainty, and the human approver. Drafting is read-only; creation requires explicit approval.
