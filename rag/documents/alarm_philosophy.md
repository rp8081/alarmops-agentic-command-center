# Enterprise Alarm Philosophy

## Priority and recurrence
Priority is based on consequence, time to respond, and asset criticality—not event frequency alone. Repeated occurrences of the same alarm should trigger a rationalization review when the recurrence threshold is reached. Review the root cause, setpoint, deadband, delay, suppression, and operator action.

## Nuisance alarm safeguards
Never automatically shelve or suppress an alarm based only on an AI recommendation. Rationalization candidates require documented engineering review and approval. Each conclusion should retain the alarm identifiers, maintenance records, procedure citations, time range, and tool provenance used to reach it.

## Confidence
Confidence should fall when a source times out, no relevant procedure is found, or evidence conflicts. Missing evidence must be visible to the reviewer rather than silently replaced by a guess.
