# Boiler Feed Pump 101 Operating Procedure

## Normal operating envelope
Boiler Feed Pump 101 normally maintains discharge pressure between 8.6 and 9.4 MPa. A reading below 8.2 MPa for more than thirty seconds is abnormal. Confirm any low reading against the redundant pressure indicator before changing equipment state.

## Low discharge pressure response
Check feedwater demand, suction header pressure, suction strainer differential pressure, recirculation-valve position, and the pressure-transmitter impulse line. If verified pressure continues to fall, notify the control-room lead and start Boiler Feed Pump 102 under the approved standby-pump startup procedure. Do not bypass an interlock.

## Escalation boundary
Trip, bypass, isolation, or switching actions remain operator decisions. The decision-support system may retrieve evidence and draft a ticket, but it must not execute a plant-control action.
